# Preview Radar Rules

Preview Radar tracks official and reviewable pre-release card information.

## Allowed Sources

- Official Bandai/Gundam Card Game product pages.
- Official Bandai/Gundam Card Game news pages.
- Official social profiles when source URLs are captured.
- Manual imports from trusted community reports must remain marked for review.

## Status Values

- `Rumor`
- `Leak nao confirmado`
- `Preview oficial`
- `Texto parcial`
- `Texto confirmado`
- `Imagem confirmada`
- `Aguardando traducao`
- `Traduzido`
- `Adicionado ao banco`

## Rules

- Do not invent text, card names, codes, rarity, or effects.
- Keep Japanese/original text separate from provisional translation.
- Keep reviewed translation separate from provisional translation.
- A preview can be converted into a database card only after official confirmation or release matching.
- Preserve source URL and publication date.
